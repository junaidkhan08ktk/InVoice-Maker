# Invoice Generator for Small Businesses (Android)

A fast, offline-first Android invoice generator built for small businesses and freelancers in India.  
Designed to create GST-compliant invoices in under 30 seconds — without signup, ads, or unnecessary complexity.

This app focuses on speed, reliability, and legal correctness, making it suitable for real-world business use and Google Play Store distribution.

---

## 🚀 Features

### Core Functionality
- Create GST-compliant invoices and estimates
- Automatic CGST / SGST / IGST calculation
- Professional PDF invoice generation
- Share invoices via WhatsApp, email, or download
- Offline-first (no internet required)
- No login or account creation

### Business Management
- Business profile with logo and GSTIN
- Customer and item management
- Auto invoice numbering
- Paid / unpaid invoice tracking
- Monthly sales summary

### Invoice Templates
- Classic GST invoice (default)
- Modern minimal design
- Simple / thermal printer-friendly layout

## 🇮🇳 GST Compliance

Invoices generated by this app include:
- Business name and address
- GSTIN
- Unique invoice number
- Invoice date
- Customer details
- Detailed tax breakup (CGST / SGST / IGST)
- Total payable amount

> Disclaimer: This app is a billing utility and does not provide legal or tax advice.
